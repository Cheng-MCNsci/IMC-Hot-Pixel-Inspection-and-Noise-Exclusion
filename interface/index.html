Intensity bin boundaries

Enter three positive, increasing bin boundaries in raw intensity units. Settings start blank for each marker. The exploratory threshold and display maximum are separate from the working cutoff recorded below.

Outline colors: ■ Yellow: cell under review. ■ Cyan: other cells in the review population (neurons in this example). ■ Magenta: cells outside that population (non-neuronal cells in this example). Turn off Show all cell outlines in crop to isolate the reviewed cell. Colors follow your input population flags; they are not an automatic cell-type prediction. High visibility keeps a dark border for contrast.

Total cell signal is the sum of all raw selected-marker pixel intensities in the cell. Whole-cell mean is that total divided by all cell pixels, including zeros.

For either displayed range, contribution to mean = the range’s intensity sum ÷ all cell pixels; % of total cell signal = the range’s intensity sum ÷ total cell signal × 100. These express the same contribution in raw mean-intensity units and as a percentage.

Example and boundary rule

A cell with total signal 56 across 155 pixels has mean 0.36129. A range with intensity sum 10 contributes 0.06452 to that mean, or 17.86% of the cell’s signal. Bins include their upper boundary: 3 < I ≤ 10 includes pixels equal to 10; I > 10 excludes them.

Review changes save when you leave a field or select Save review. Viewing alone does not mark a cell reviewed. Export all markers saves every marker’s settings, cutoff decisions and annotations. Import reviews merges matching tutorial records, keeping newer timestamps.
