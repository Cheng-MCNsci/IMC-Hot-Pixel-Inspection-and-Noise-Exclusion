Connectivity: hot pixels connect through sides or corners (8-connected); no gaps are filled. The gallery minimum only filters which complete components are shown.

Distances: shortest edge-to-edge gaps between full pixel-square masks, using your X/Y calibration. Overlap, edge touching and corner touching have gap zero. The nearby-cell gap filters table rows, not the rule flags. When “All cell masks in crop” is off, only nearby-cell outlines are shown.

Flag names: area = full mask below the area limit; small-hot = too many pixels in small components; cross-marker = count limit exceeded in enough markers; large-gap = too close to a large component; no-pixels = no non-hot pixels remain. Cross-marker-pending means some required cutoffs are missing.

These are inspection flags. No cell is deleted and no corrected intensity is calculated. Continue to Chapter 4 for noise-spread review; final retained-pixel quantification is still to come.
